Addendum: the number was 807, then 612 — and why I published both

Sobre: El hackeo de $6.3B no fue un bug de código (Dev.to, id 4949662, publicado 30-Oct-2026...

securitydevopsopensourcegithub

About: "The $6.3B hack was not a code bug" (Dev.to, id 4949662, published 30-Oct-2026 21:58:55Z). Fingerprint: HUELLA-ADDENDUM-CORRECCION-MOTOR-20261030

The article states that 807 of the observations across ten repositories are not 807 vulnerabilities, and names 228 of them as coming from two codes of the instrument itself that detect the mechanism, not the defect. That paragraph was honest and still is. What was missing was the next step: if the code detects the mechanism and not the defect, the code was wrong. I fixed it. This addendum publishes the exact figure, because silently correcting an already published number is the same class of fraud as inflating it.

What was wrong (two domain defects, not two thresholds)

1. GATE_RC_4_SECRETO_EXPUESTO measured usage, not leakage. It flagged ${{ secrets.* }} in env: — global or job level — and in with:. Those are the two sanctioned mechanisms for handing a secret to a step without letting it into the body of the script: flagging them as a finding was marking the correct way to use it as a leak. Domain corrected: RC 4 measures the interpolation inside run:, the only case where the value enters a shell line and can end up in a log.

2. GATE_RC_2_SUMIDERO_IMPUDO treated the absence of download-artifact as if it were the sink. A job with id-token: write that materialises no tree, invokes no dynamic tool and restores no cache executes nothing: the invariant held vacuously and the finding was the shape of a job that consumes nothing. Domain corrected: RC 2 measures the presence of the tree or of its dependencies (checkout, npx/pipx, or actions/cache instead of the artifact — the cache is not authenticated against the build of the same run, and that is a broken chain of custody).

Removed: 195 observations (149 from RC 4, 46 from RC 2). Not 228: 228 was the size of the class, 195 its contribution — the rest of the class was already falling out through other conjunctions. The codes that hold the thesis up (RC 1, RC 5, SC1_ROOT_WRITE_INHERITED) did not move: the correction removed noise, not criteria.

The delta is not estimated: the 163 workflows of the batch were loaded once, anchored by blob_sha + sha256, and were run against the previous engine and the corrected one in the same run.

And the headline shrinks while the thesis gets stronger

  • 807 observations, 115 with the conjunction → 14.2 %.
  • 612 observations, 115 with the conjunction → 18.8 %.

The proportion of what the article defends went up 4.6 points because noise was removed from the denominator. The falsifier did not move: 10 out of 10 repositories still have at least one authority conjunction, and the three readings of the falsifier (A 10/10, B 6/10, C 4/10) remain identical. What changed is the instrument; the thesis still stands on the same code as before the correction.

The method, so that it can be repeated or taken apart

  1. Pre-registered prediction before touching the engine — the independent census of the surface predicted RC 4 = 4 and RC 2 = 30. Measured: RC 4 = 3 (the census counted job rows; RC 4 emits one finding per job, and a job in withastro/astro had two steps with a secret), RC 2 = 30. The prediction missed by one and the miss is published in the record, not deleted.
  2. Reproduction of the 807 as a control over the same bytes before correcting: 596 + 211 = 807, exact. An engine that does not reproduce its own published figure cannot measure its correction.
  3. The test bench was part of the defect. The positive case of RC 4 asserted RC 4 on env: — that is, the bench endorsed the defect. It was rewritten, and three negative controls that make the old behaviour fail were added: sanctioned env: → 0 findings, sanctioned with: → 0, job without a tree, dynamic tool or cache → 0. --self-test: 8 → 11 cases, RC 0.
  4. Residue declared, not hidden: a job with env: carrying secrets that executes third-party code only falls under RC 1 if it also declares write scopes; outside that case the combination stays OUT OF MEASUREMENT. The narrowing is accepted in exchange for removing 195 observations that were not a defect.

The rate that was missing

The TPR of RC 2 and RC 4 is already measured, and measured on the current engine. It is not a sample: it is a census of the whole class, because the residual population was 33 findings and sampling 20 of 30 would have saved nothing.

Before opening a single job body, a pre-registered prediction was written, so that the result would not be post-hoc. Then the 163 workflow files of the batch were re-downloaded and their sha256 verified against the manifest: 163 identical, 0 failures. The corrected engine reproduced 401 exact gate findings over those bytes (RC 1 60 · RC 2 30 · RC 4 3 · RC 5 308). The population is reproduced, not remembered.

The negative control is the part that matters and the part almost nobody publishes: without it, a high TPR only proves that the reader agrees with itself.

The figure that cannot be published as "vulnerabilities" is 612. Of the 30 residual RC 2 findings, 20 emit only the actions/checkout reason — and actions/checkout next to id-token: write is the shape of every correct OIDC workflow: the checkout is a requirement of the pattern, not its defect. The false positive did not disappear with the correction: it changed form. Before, the engine read badly (228 spurious observations); now it reads well a shape that is not a defect. Saying "612 vulnerabilities" would still be an error, and this addendum does not say it.

And 16 of the 33 (48 %) are a finding of another class, which the prediction itself had not foreseen: NO_SUMIDERO. The job declares id-token: write and no step consumes that authority. It does not publish, does not sign, does not attest. The authority is granted and not exercised. The taxonomy of the prediction did not have that class; it is declared as a gap in the prediction, not as a finding forced into place.

That class is not assigned a severity, and the reason is declared: its value is NOT_MEASURED. We do not know whether it is worth 0 — the authority granted to a job that does not use it is surface without an act, and surface is not a defect by itself — nor whether it is the strongest finding of the batch: a short-lived OIDC token that nobody consumes is still a credential issued against the registry, and least privilege is measured by what the credential can do, not by what the YAML uses. Both readings are defensible on the same bytes and neither is measured. Writing a severity here would be inventing it; writing 0 would be reading not measured as zero. It stays open and in plain sight, in the same document that does close the figures it could close.

Two defects of the reader were found and corrected before publishing any figure. The first gave 45/46 on the negative control, because a comment at the end of a job key (test-dev: # TODO) broke the anchor. The second gave 30/33 in the formal census, because a comment at the end of a permission (id-token: write # OIDC) broke the test. Both, had they been published, would have "proven" that the engine was wrong when the error belonged to whoever read it. They remain written here because the path counts.

What is still unmeasured does not change shape, only size: RC 1 (60), RC 5 (308) and the SC1 codes (211) still have no rate. And the four blind spots of the instrument are still there: default permissions of GITHUB_TOKEN, OIDC policies of the destination registry, native code not isolated at runtime, and reusable workflows hosted in foreign repositories.
